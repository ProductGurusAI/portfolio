The studio

The studio started as a way to answer one question properly: is this worth building? The method that came out of it is short. Talk to the people who would pay. Size the market with sources you can check. Work out the unit economics. Then say build, wait, or no, in writing, with the evidence attached. Since then the same method has been used on shift scheduling for a retail chain, an internal audit tool, two marketplace concepts, and two hardware ideas.

Seven products have come out of it so far: three production platforms, three ventures taken through full discovery, and one internal tool that started it all because something at work was broken and nobody else was going to open a spec doc about it. In 2026 the studio opened the same research to small business owners: the café, clinic, and shop decisions that deserve the homework a startup would do. See the services →

How we work

Five rules we don't bend

They cost us the occasional project. They've never cost a client one.

01 · Evidence

Every number has a source

Market sizes are footnoted. Interview counts are real. If a figure on this site can't be traced to a case study or a public source, it doesn't go on the site.

02 · Honesty

"Don't build it" is a valid answer

Two of the seven products were re-scoped or paused after research. We'd rather lose the build work than watch you spend on something the interviews already killed.

03 · Ownership

You own everything we make

Interview notes, findings, user stories, the site files, the analytics accounts. All of it sits in your name from day one, so leaving us is never a hostage situation.

04 · Scope

We say what we don't do

No e-commerce stores. No paid ads. No "we can do anything." When a job needs a specialist we don't have, we'll say so on the first call and point you to one.

05 · Plain language

Deliverables you can read on a phone

A findings deck you can act on without a translator. A brief a developer can quote from. A site audit ranked by what to fix first. If it needs a meeting to understand, we rewrite it.
