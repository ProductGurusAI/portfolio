The shapes

Seven ways we work with clients

Timelines are typical, not guaranteed. A busy interview calendar or a slow host can add a week, and we tell you when it does.

Research sprint

Typical2 weeks

What goes inCustomer or staff interviews, or desk research when interviews aren't possible.

What comes outA findings deck and a written build, wait or no recommendation.

Who it suitsAnyone about to spend on a build, a lease or a launch.

Prototype

Typical3–4 weeks

What goes inA decision already made, plus access to a handful of real users.

What comes outA clickable prototype tested with those users.

Who it suitsFounders who need to show something before engineers get involved.

Sprint + prototype

Typical5–6 weeks

What goes inA question and a rough idea.

What comes outFindings deck, recommendation and a tested prototype.

Who it suitsOwners who want to go from "should we?" to "here's what to build" in one run.

Fractional product owner

TypicalMonthly, part-time

What goes inA build already running, and nobody on your side owning it.

What comes outA groomed backlog, a roadmap and someone who says no to scope creep.

Who it suitsSmall companies who can't justify a full-time product hire.

Website

Typical2–3 weeks

What goes inYour services, your photos and a call about who the site is for.

What comes outA fast service site with analytics and search set up, on a host you control.

Who it suitsService businesses whose site is slow, dated or missing.

Website audit

Typical1 week

What goes inThe URL of your existing site.

What comes outA scored technical report and a prioritised fix list.

Who it suitsAnyone who suspects their site is holding them back.

Local SEO setup

Typical2 weeks, then optional monthly

What goes inYour business details, service area and listings access.

What comes outListings, citations and on-page basics set up and verified.

Who it suitsBusinesses that serve a neighbourhood and don't show up in its searches.

How pricing works

Fixed price for fixed scope

Quoted after a call

Every price comes out of the 30-minute call, in writing. We don't publish prices because two research sprints rarely need the same number of interviews.

Fixed-scope work

Sprints, prototypes, websites, audits and local SEO setup are fixed price. The scope is written down before you pay. If it changes, the price changes in writing too.

Retainers

The fractional product owner and the optional local SEO follow-on are monthly. Stop at the end of any month.

What you won't find here

No hourly rates, no "starting from" numbers and no tiered packages.

Questions we get on the first call

Before you book

What happens after the first call?

Within a couple of days you get a written scope: what we'd do, what you'd receive, the typical timeline and a fixed price. If it fits, we book a start date. If it doesn't, keep the scope; it's useful on its own.

Can we start small?

Yes. A website audit is one week and a research sprint is two. Both stand on their own, and both tell you whether a bigger engagement is worth it.

Do you work remotely, or outside Vancouver?

Yes. We're based in Vancouver and work with clients across Canada remotely. Interviews and workshops run over video.

Who owns the deliverables?

You do. Findings decks, briefs, prototypes, site code and accounts are yours once the invoice is settled. Take a brief to any developer or a site to any host.

What don't you do?

We don't build e-commerce stores and we don't run paid ads. If those are what you need, we'll say so on the call and point you elsewhere.
