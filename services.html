How the work runs

Five stages, whichever service you pick

A research sprint stops after Decide. A build brief adds Define. A website or a prototype goes through Build. Local SEO is the Grow stage on its own. Timelines on this page are typical, and every engagement is quoted after a call.

Discover

Interviews with customers or staff, desk research and benchmarking. We find out what's true today.

Decide

Market sizing, unit economics and feasibility, ending in a build, wait or no recommendation.

Define

Process maps, user stories with acceptance criteria, a PRD (product requirements document: the single page a developer builds from) and a vendor pick.

Build

A prototype or a site, then UAT (user acceptance testing: real people trying it before it ships).

Grow

Analytics, search and local presence, so the thing you built gets used.
